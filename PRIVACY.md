# Privacy

**This site doesn't track you.**

- No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no fingerprinting.
- No requests to other companies when a page loads. Fonts and libraries are served from this site.
- Your theme and sound choices are kept in your own browser's storage and never leave it.
- The booking calendar (cal.com) loads only if you click to book a call; cal.com's own privacy policy
  applies from that point.
- The contact letter opens your own email app. Nothing you type is sent anywhere by this site.
- Pages send no referrer when you follow a link away from the site.

GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## About Vyom's own data

- No phone number is published anywhere, including the web resume.
- The email address isn't written in the page source; it's put together only when a visitor asks for it.
- Photos are published without Exif or location data.
- Nothing confidential from current or past employers appears on the site.
